# Implementation Plan

Status: **IMPLEMENTATION PLAN. Revision 7.2 compatibility (owner decision 008 adds `detected_corruption_halts_the_run` to cut 3), 2026-11-05.** Admission standing is recorded in `docs/decisions/` (records 007 and 008), not here. A plan authorizes work only through those records, after the governing documents themselves have been admitted at exact digests. This plan maps every cut to the governing documents and the tests that verify it; it decides no semantic question.

## Rules for every cut

- Bounded by this plan; the Constitutional Delta is `ΔO = NONE, ΔS = NONE,
  ΔA = NONE` unless stated; a cut that discovers an undeclared fact,
  carrier, writer, selector or transition stops and returns to Audit.
- A cut's tests are the ones named here by their exact names in
  [testing_contract.md](../testing_contract.md); a test name that is not in
  the contract is a plan defect. A cut requires only tests whose inputs its
  own and earlier cuts produce; no cut requires a later cut's files.
- Every governed entry point and every test process runs under the
  supported execution contract (constitution §12): `python -B`, no
  `__pycache__` inside `elevator/`.
- Verification evidence is the executed test command with counts, never
  prose. Each cut writes an implementation record under
  `docs/implementation/` classifying OSA and CPOC applicability, and
  updates `current_work.md`.
- **Staged standing.** A cut reaches `IMPLEMENTED / LOCAL VERIFY PASS` when
  its own tests pass. A cut whose CPOC applicability is `REQUIRED` carries
  the suffix `/ CPOC PENDING` until the witness of cut 10 has run and
  passed for the code identity that includes the cut; it then receives
  `/ CPOC-QUALIFIED`. Cuts 11 and 12 may begin on `CPOC PENDING` cuts;
  nothing may be called complete for the project, published, or presented
  as continuity-proven until cuts 3, 9 and 11 are `CPOC-QUALIFIED`.
- No cut may introduce a dependency outside architecture §6, a module
  outside architecture §8 without a note in its record, or a carrier outside
  authority §6. No cut implements a later cut's feature early: a CLI
  command whose module arrives later is absent, not stubbed.
- Fixtures: each cut commits the Traces it introduces with their hashes;
  expected outputs are evidence and are updated only with a note explaining
  the behavioural change and the governing section that permits it.
- Time log: each cut records time spent in `current_work.md` §7.

## Cross-cut obligations

- **OSA applicability**: cuts 3 and 9 are `REQUIRED` (they write governed
  facts); cuts 1, 2, 4 to 8 and 11 are `REQUIRED` for the facts they
  construct, project or report; cut 10 is evidence; cut 12 `NOT APPLICABLE`.
- **CPOC applicability** is stated per cut above. `NOT APPLICABLE` always
  carries its bounded reason in the implementation record.
- **Completion standing of the project**: cuts 3, 9 and 11
  `CPOC-QUALIFIED`; every other cut `IMPLEMENTED / LOCAL VERIFY PASS`; the
  witness record present with every receipt in a Store-bound Operational
  Log; the README commands executed.
- **Cut-local dependency rule**: the test list of every cut names only
  tests whose inputs that cut and earlier cuts produce; the cpoc §6 "Cut"
  column is the authority for where each negative witness runs.

## What admits this plan

A decision record in `docs/decisions/`, written by the owner after the
governing documents have been admitted by a prior record at exact digests,
and after this plan has been inspected against that admitted revision.
Admission of the plan authorizes cuts in order; it does not authorize
skipping a cut's tests or reordering authority-affecting cuts (3, 9, 10)
relative to each other.
